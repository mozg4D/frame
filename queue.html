Task queue

What is being worked on, what comes next, and what is still being discussed. Priorities can change as the work reveals new constraints.

Updated 2 October 2026, 14:30 UTC

Current work

In progress

Save File choices and mobile number entry are under development. First-perimeter improvements are released in r145.

  • Save filename and format choice

    Give each existing supported format its own row and enter the base filename separately. Ensure the applied extension and saved file contents match the selected format. Native browser format-picker limitations are under investigation; no new formats are planned for this task.

  • Mobile number entry

    The first tap into a numeric field selects the whole number for quick replacement. Preserve drag and scrub gestures and intentional editing.

Next

Investigations

Directions to evaluate after the current perimeter work; scope and order may change.

  • Clean up the section before offsetting

    Investigate outline-aware cleanup of the model section while retaining holes, thin features, and meaningful corners.

  • Compare an alternative offset approach

    Explore an alternative outline calculation and compare its geometry and cost with the current approach. A replacement is not yet promised.

  • Lower-priority improvements

    Broaden tests of functions and their combinations, then pursue speed and code-size improvements where measurements support them.

Decisions & discussion

Future proposals

The behaviors below need design and testing. They are not implemented in the current slicer.

  • Nominal width first

    Plan paths around nominal bead width, with variable widths considered for exceptional situations rather than as the default.

  • Acute corners and residual gaps

    Work out routes for corners below 45°, tiny remaining gaps, and the extrusion transitions needed to enter and leave those regions.

  • Boolean reliability

    Known Boolean issues remain under investigation. Diagnostic findings are not shipped fixes.
