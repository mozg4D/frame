Task queue

Unfinished work and ideas for Frame. Priorities can change as development and testing reveal new constraints.

Updated 10 October 2026 · Current application: r226

Current work

In progress

  • Large-scene editing

    Reduce heavy component-drag and cold-selection latency while preserving source identity, authored precision, Undo/Redo, and save/export behavior. The GPU authoring development source still needs regression and browser acceptance before activation.

  • Adaptive perimeter placement and flow

    Broaden source-boundary-based paths for thin regions, acute corners, residual gaps, curved or branched channels, through-necks, and inward spirals. Keep nominal width as the default, distinguish commanded flow from nozzle geometry, and preserve outside/hole ordering and whole-layer overlap checks. Broader coverage remains to be verified.

  • First-perimeter point cleanup

    Reduce redundant trajectory points across more topology and feature combinations. Keep cumulative deviation within 2% of nominal line width against the original unoptimized inset, including acute corners. Preserve thin features and topology without new axis crossings or self-intersections. Bead coverage and extrusion remain separate checks.

  • Import, slice, and print

    Verify the complete workflow on a real printer. Software geometry checks and angle warnings alone do not establish printed coverage, support, or bridge behavior. Unfinished slicer sources and tools are in slicer-lab.

Next

Investigations

  • Source-first section and inset

    Investigate section cleanup and direct analytic first insets while retaining untouched source boundaries, holes, thin features, genuine offset splits, and the cumulative 2% trajectory limit. Compare correctness and complete Slice time on Ring, hourglass, and shallow-connector cases.

  • AI API workflow

    Check modeling, geometry repair, Undo, and saving end to end. Resolve differences between enabled geometry, viewport visibility, and printer visibility; keep additions essential and tested.

  • Performance and resource use

    Measure complete operations and peak memory on representative scenes. Expand function-combination coverage, investigate costly repeated work, and check for regressions alongside any speed or code-size improvement.

Ideas

To evaluate

These proposals need design and testing; listing them does not indicate completion.

  • Flat ends and corner extrusion

    Evaluate end caps, acute-tip entry and exit, local flow transitions, and deposited-bead overlap separately from centerline accuracy.

  • Boolean reliability
