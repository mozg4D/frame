# W-long transitions through an original neck

Release: r189. Base: main r188, 0bcbbdff01b50aac685bb0dda3ac7b1bd7b70616.
Worker SHA-256: f118c25d1f599f2be33458b4567441d85a8d094bcd1befb7797c5469690e0f58.

A bounded original neck wider than W and narrower than 1.6W can now form one continuous open route around its two lobes and through the neck. The route starts at W, expands over an actual axis length of at least W toward 1.6W where space permits, narrows to the original finite neck, expands after the neck, then returns to W over at least W. Any geometric reduction follows source containment; width is never changed to manufacture a spiral. The new feature is independent of acute restart placement.

## Admission and geometry

Admission requires one original material ring, 12–64 original source vertices, one nominal outer fallback path with at most 96 vertices, count 1, complete current face incidence and complete finite source-bank ownership. Both banks must satisfy the retained finite monotone profile. Every new segment is checked for a fixed nominal nozzle radius W/2 with exact binary rational segment-to-source clearance, zero-tolerance flat-ribbon containment, nonlocal axis crossings, foreign source contact and printed-neighbor contact. Feed width equals display width, stays W–1.6W, and both independent ends are W. Source coordinates, outer axis extrema and clockwise lobe traversal remain unchanged; retained original directed intervals inherit their original Float32 angles. New intervals use the maximum current original-face inclination on their owned source-bank intervals.

The helper prepares geometry only. The owner revalidates the actual path, phase lengths, widths, source identity, original CW path, clearance, ribbon containment and independently recomputed rational overlap ledger. Invalid, incomplete, stale or oversized evidence falls back to the original routes without dropping a model. Two actual helper mutation campaigns (sub-W start and false overlap ledger) preserve all 363 original layers, references and angles.

Existing accepted spiral and neck-continuity routes keep their priority and complete layer budget. The new route is skipped on those layers. This preserves previously shipped legal continuity; it does not displace a retained route merely to add a profile. New seam charge conservatively sums every final pair involving a nonidentical ribbon, cancels unchanged common pairs once, and gives no credit for removed old overlap. The whole-layer ceiling remains 0.5W². Ordinary independent seams retain the established 0.5W policy.
